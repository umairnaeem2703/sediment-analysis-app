# Project Goal #

* To calculate total suspended and bedload sediment volumes to analyze existing sediment accumulation, its impact on reservoir fill rates, and the resulting effects on the dam's operational lifespan.



# Project Milestones #

* **Milestone 1: Regional Data Ingestion (Phase 1):** Load and organize regional basin parameters, such as sub-basin areas and main waterway slopes, into the database to minimize manual entry.


* **Milestone 2: Suspended Sediment Math Engine (Phase 2):** Calculate gross soil loss and apply the Sediment Delivery Ratio (SIO) to determine the exact mass and volume of suspended sediment reaching the reservoir. This phase strictly relies on numerical inputs and mathematical yield metrics, leaving the 5-class RGB spatial mapping as an end-deliverable for the hydrologist/GIS analyst.


* **Milestone 3: Bedload Transport Modeling (Phase 3):** Utilize the Wilcock & Crowe fractional model alongside daily river flow data to calculate the volume of heavy bedload sediment moving along the river bottom.


* **Milestone 4: Yield Integration & Bathymetry (Phase 4):** Add the static suspended sediment volume to the dynamic annual bedload volume to find the combined total sediment load. Map these total volumes to the individual dam's bathymetry curve to calculate precise physical sediment elevations.


* **Milestone 5: Final Analysis & Reporting:** Produce a summary dataset that isolates the extreme minimum and maximum total sediment volume years to inform dam management.
